🧑‍🍳 Module 02

Pre-Event Prep

Complete these steps before the event so both recipes — each batched to 2 gallons — are ready to finish and serve on site. The remaining steps happen at the station, at the event.

Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water — prep ahead

  • 8 large cucumbers (reserve 1 for garnish)
  • 8 bunches fresh mint (reserve 2 bunches for garnish)
  • 8 limes (reserve 3 for garnish)
  • 64 oz (8 cups) cold water
  1. Before slicing, set aside 1 cucumber, 2 bunches of mint, and 3 limes for onsite garnish (see below).
  2. Wash and thinly slice the remaining 7 cucumbers.
  3. Thinly slice the remaining 5 limes.
  4. Lightly bruise the remaining 6 bunches of mint leaves by gently pressing or clapping them between your hands to release their aroma.
  5. In the dispenser, combine the cucumber slices, mint, and lime slices. Add the cold water.
🧊
Hold here until service

Keep the base chilled in the dispenser.

Pineapple Ginger Sparkler — prep ahead

  • 1 (64 oz) container pineapple juice
  • About 2 cups jarred lime juice
  • 17 oz (about half of the 32 oz bottle) ginger simple syrup
  1. In the dispenser, combine the pineapple juice, jarred lime juice, and ginger simple syrup. Stir well.
  2. Chill the mixture until cold.
🍍
Hold here until service

Keep the base chilled in the dispenser.

Garnish — bring supplies onsite

Garnish is prepped on site rather than ahead of time, using the produce reserved during Cucumber Mint prep: 1 cucumber and 2 bunches of mint for the Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water, and 3 limes and mint for the Pineapple Ginger Sparkler. Only US Wellness staff should prepare garnishes. Clean the knife and cutting board and pack them to bring to the event.

🚐
Bring to the event
  • Both prepped recipe bases, chilled in the dispensers
  • Reserved garnish produce: 1 cucumber, 2 bunches of mint, and 3 limes, kept whole and unsliced until Station Setup
  • Remaining ginger simple syrup — bring extra to offer as an optional sweetener for the Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water
  • Knife and cutting board, cleaned, for on-site garnish prep
  • Muddlers, for any on-site touch-ups
  • 3 (10 lb) bags of ice, total for both recipes
  • Other supplies provided by US Wellness — table cloth, gloves, trash bags, pop-up trash can, sign displays, and poster
📋 Module 03

Station Setup

Set up the station before doors open, following this order.

Setup order

  1. Lay down the plastic table cloth first — it protects the table for the full event.
  2. Place the two 2-gallon dispensers on the table, with the Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water on one side and the Pineapple Ginger Sparkler on the other, spigots facing out toward guests for easy pouring.
  3. Set out prep tools — small knife, cutting board for garnishes.
  4. Prep the garnish — slice cucumber wheels and lime wheels, and rinse mint sprigs — and keep it near the dispensers, ready to serve.
  5. Stack the plastic cups next to the dispensers.
  6. Set up signage — a recipe card in each of the 2 clear (7x5) sign displays, and the 11x8.5 poster where guests see it first.
  7. Stage cleanup supplies — pop-up trash can, gloves, paper towels, and trash bags within arm's reach of the station.

Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water — finish onsite

  1. Add the sparkling water (3 (2-liter) bottles).
  2. Stir gently to combine without losing carbonation.
  3. Serve over ice in cups, making sure each one gets some cucumber, mint, and lime.

The batched base already includes sliced cucumber, mint, and lime — a fresh garnish on top finishes the pour.

  • Slice a few extra thin cucumber wheels and mint sprigs to have on hand.
  • Add a cucumber wheel or mint sprig to each cup as you pour, so every serving looks freshly made.

Pineapple Ginger Sparkler — finish onsite

  1. Gently stir in the sparkling water (about 2 ½ (2-liter) bottles) to keep it fizzy.
  2. Fill cups with ice.
  3. Pour about ½ cup of the pineapple mixture per cup.
  4. Garnish with lime slices or mint.

Garnish (lime and mint) is prepped on site, right before service.

  • Slice lime wheels and rinse mint sprigs.
  • Garnish each cup with a lime slice or mint sprig as you pour.

🥤 Module 04

Serving Guests & Station Flow

Keep both drinks stocked, cold, and clearly labeled through the whole event.

Running the station

  • Place each recipe card near the dispenser, so guests know what they're pouring.
  • Glove up before handling ice, garnish, or refilling a dispenser.
  • Use the muddlers and knife/cutting board to prep garnish.
  • Wipe down the table and restock cups regularly throughout the shift.

Wellness Talking Points: Hydration

  • Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water: cucumbers are about 96% water, mint is refreshing and often associated with digestion, and lime adds a boost of vitamin C.
  • Pineapple Ginger Sparkler: pineapple contains bromelain, ginger is commonly used to help settle the stomach, and lime adds a boost of vitamin C.
  • Staying hydrated throughout the day supports energy, focus, and mood.
💬
Keep it casual

These are general wellness talking points, not medical advice — share them conversationally and avoid specific health claims.

🍯
Optional sweetener

Bring extra ginger simple syrup and keep it on the side at the station. Offer it to guests who'd like to sweeten their Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water.

🗑️
Trash management
  • Pop-up trash can stays within reach of the station at all times
  • Swap liners once ¾ full — never let trash overflow onto the table
🧤
Hygiene reminder

Never scoop ice or garnish with bare hands. Change gloves if they touch anything other than the station's own supplies.

📦 Module 05

Breakdown & Cleanup

End-of-event teardown — leave the station clean and ready to return.

  1. Empty and rinse both dispensers; wipe down the muddlers, knife, and cutting board.
  2. Discard the plastic table cloth.
  3. Bag remaining trash, tie it off, and collapse the pop-up trash can.
  4. Return to the USW office: pack of gloves, the pop-up trash can, both clear sign displays, and the 11x8.5 horizontal display.
📦
Everything else

All other materials — recipe ingredients and equipment, plus anything else ordered from Amazon — can be discarded, kept, or donated. Please do not leave anything onsite at the client site.

📖 Module 06

Full Recipe - Reference

Full scaled recipes for both drinks as a reference.

Cucumber Mint Sparkling Water

Ingredients

  • 8 large cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 8 bunches fresh mint, lightly bruised
  • 8 limes, thinly sliced
  • 3 (2-liter) bottles sparkling water, chilled
  • 64 oz (8 cups) cold water
  • Ice, from the shared 3 (10 lb) bag supply for both drinks
Yield: 50 (5 oz) servings (2 gallons) 5 cal · 2g carbs / serving

Instructions

  1. Wash and thinly slice the cucumbers.
  2. Thinly slice the limes.
  3. Lightly bruise the mint leaves by gently pressing or clapping them between your hands to release their aroma.
  4. In the dispenser, combine the cucumber slices, mint, and lime slices. Add the cold water.
  5. Just before serving, add the sparkling water.
  6. Stir gently to combine without losing carbonation.
  7. Serve over ice in cups, making sure each one gets some cucumber, mint, and lime.

Pineapple Ginger Sparkler

Ingredients

  • 1 (64 oz) container pineapple juice
  • About 2 cups jarred lime juice
  • 17 oz (about half of the 32 oz bottle) ginger simple syrup
  • About 2 ½ (2-liter) bottles (156 oz) sparkling water
  • Ice, from the shared 3 (10 lb) bag supply for both drinks
  • Lime slices or fresh mint for garnish (prepped on site)
Yield: 50 (5 oz) servings (2 gallons) 55 cal · 14g carbs / serving

Instructions

  1. In the dispenser, combine the pineapple juice, jarred lime juice, and ginger simple syrup. Stir well.
  2. Chill the mixture until cold.
  3. Just before serving, gently stir in the sparkling water to keep it fizzy.
  4. Fill cups with ice.
  5. Pour about ½ cup of the pineapple mixture per cup.
  6. Garnish with lime slices or mint.
